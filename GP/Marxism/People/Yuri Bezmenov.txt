The Four Stages of Ideological Subversion
1. Demoralization (15 to 20 years)
The longest and most crucial phase.
Aims to reshape the values and worldview of a generation through education, media, and cultural institutions.
Blurs the lines between right and wrong so society loses its moral compass and ability to assess facts objectively.
2. Destabilization (2 to 5 years)
Focuses on core structural elements: foreign relations, defense, and the economy.
Exploits existing social, economic, and racial divisions to create widespread chaos and institutional distrust. 
3. Crisis (2 to 6 weeks)
The tipping point where accumulated instability boils over into a violent shift in power or a national emergency.
Results in a radical restructuring of the government and economy. 
4. Normalization (Decades or permanent)
The final stage where an authoritarian regime takes control, promising to restore order and "normalize" society.
Eliminates dissent and solidifies absolute power under the guise of stability
